# Tarmių vartojimas - Lliçó Pimsleur B1

---

This is a Lithuanian conversation lesson (B1 level) based on the dialogue about the use of dialects and social attitudes (*Tarmių vartojimas ir požiūris į jas*). Listen carefully and repeat aloud when prompted.

---

### Part 1: Asking about dialect use

How do you say in Lithuanian: “Do you speak in your native dialect in the village or in the city?”

> **Ar tu mieste kalbi savo gimtąja tarme?**

Let's break that down.

First, listen to the phrase for “native dialect”:
> **gimtoji tarmė**

How do you say “in your native dialect”?
> **savo gimtąja tarme**

And the complete sentence:
> **Ar tu mieste kalbi savo gimtąja tarme?**

Now listen to how to respond: “During classes I try to use the standard language.”
> **Per paskaitas stengiuosi vartoti bendrinę kalbą.**

---

### Part 2: Public attitudes and perception

How do you say “In public, everyone says that dialects need to be protected”?

> **Viešai visi sako, kad tarmes reikia saugoti.**

Let's break that down.

“In public”:
> **viešai**

“Dialects need to be protected”:
> **tarmes reikia saugoti**

And the complete sentence:
> **Viešai visi sako, kad tarmes reikia saugoti.**

Listen to the contrast: “However, studies show that people receive unwanted attention.”
> **Tačiau tyrimai rodo, kad žmonės sulaukia nepageidaujamo dėmesio.**

---

### Part 3: Stereotypes and official settings

How do you ask: “Is it true that a person who speaks in a dialect is considered less educated?”

> **Ar tiesa, kad tarmiškai kalbantis žmogus laikomas ne taip išsilavinusiu?**

Let's break that down.

“A person who speaks in a dialect”:
> **tarmiškai kalbantis žmogus**

“Is considered less educated”:
> **laikomas ne taip išsilavinusiu**

And the complete question:
> **Ar tiesa, kad tarmiškai kalbantis žmogus laikomas ne taip išsilavinusiu?**

And the answer: “Therefore, in an official setting, people choose the standard language.”
> **Todėl oficialioje aplinkoje žmonės renkasi bendrinę kalbą.**

---

### Part 4: The emotional value of dialect

How do you say “The dialect helps to express feelings and emotions better”?

> **Tarmė padeda geriau perteikti jausmus ir emocijas.**

Let's break that down.

“To express or convey better”:
> **geriau perteikti**

“Feelings and emotions”:
> **jausmus ir emocijas**

The complete sentence:
> **Tarmė padeda geriau perteikti jausmus ir emocijas.**

And the conclusion: “In a private space among close people, the dialect brings warmth.”
> **Privačioje erdvėje tarp artimųjų tarmė suteikia šilumos.**

---

This concludes this Lithuanian lesson. Review these patterns until you can respond naturally and fluently.
