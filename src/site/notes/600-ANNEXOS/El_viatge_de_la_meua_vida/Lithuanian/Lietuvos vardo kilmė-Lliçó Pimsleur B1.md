# Lietuvos vardo kilmė - Lliçó Pimsleur B1

---

This is a Lithuanian conversation lesson (B1 level) based on the dialogue about the origin of the name Lithuania (*Lietuvos vardo kilmė*). Listen carefully and repeat aloud when prompted.

---

### Part 1: Asking about the origin of the name

How do you say in Lithuanian: “Do you know where the name Lithuania comes from?”

> **Ar žinai, iš kur kilo Lietuvos vardas?**

Let's break that down.

First, listen to the word for “name”:
> **vardas**

And “the name of Lithuania”:
> **Lietuvos vardas**

How do you say “where it originated from”?
> **iš kur kilo**

And the complete question:
> **Ar žinai, iš kur kilo Lietuvos vardas?**

Now listen to how to respond: “Yes, there are several theories.”
> **Taip, yra kelios teorijos.**

---

### Part 2: Explaining the stream theory

How do you say “The linguist Kazimieras Kuzavinis stated that the name came from the Lietava stream”?

> **Kalbininkas Kazimieras Kuzavinis teigė, kad vardas kilo iš upelio Lietava.**

Let's break that down.

How do you say “from the stream”?
> **iš upelio**

How do you say “from the Lietava stream”?
> **iš upelio Lietava**

And the complete sentence:
> **Kalbininkas Kazimieras Kuzavinis teigė, kad vardas kilo iš upelio Lietava.**

Listen to how this stream is described: “It is a small tributary of the Neris.”
> **Tai nedidelis Neries intakas.**

---

### Part 3: The militia/army hypothesis

How do you ask: “So could the name Lithuania originally have meant an army?”

> **Taigi Lietuvos vardas iš pradžių galėjo reikšti kariauną?**

Let's break that down.

How do you say “originally”?
> **iš pradžių**

How do you say “could have meant”?
> **galėjo reikšti**

And “an army/host”:
> **kariauną**

And the complete question:
> **Taigi Lietuvos vardas iš pradžių galėjo reikšti kariauną?**

And the confirming answer: “Exactly! In ancient times, Lithuanians were called leičiai.”
> **Teisingai! Lietuviai senovėje buvo vadinami leičiais.**

---

### Part 4: The popular belief about rain

How do you say “I thought that the name simply came from the word for rain”?

> **Aš maniau, kad pavadinimas kilęs tiesiog iš žodžio lietus!**

Let's break that down.

“From the word for rain”:
> **iš žodžio lietus**

“Simply”:
> **tiesiog**

The complete sentence:
> **Aš maniau, kad pavadinimas kilęs tiesiog iš žodžio lietus!**

And the conclusion: “Lithuania is a rainy country, but linguists are looking for deeper roots.”
> **Lietuva yra lietingas kraštas, bet kalbininkai ieško gilesnių šaknų.**

---

This concludes this Lithuanian lesson. Review these patterns until you can respond naturally and fluently.
